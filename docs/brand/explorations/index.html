Aqueduct: mark explorations

Eight directions for a more modern, abstract and lasting mark, each built from the same idea: a structure that carries, and what it carries. Every concept is shown as an icon on dark and light tiles, alone on light and dark, in one colour (the test of a mark that will last), at favicon sizes, and in a lockup with two wordmark options: Geist, lowercase and tight, and Geist Mono, uppercase and wide. The water uses one gradient everywhere, so it becomes the brand's signature; every mark also works without it.
