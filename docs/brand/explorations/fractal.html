Aqueduct: the Fractal mark and its typeface

You chose the Fractal direction. Below, six refinements of it, each at every size and in one colour, then the same mark (F1) set against fourteen typefaces, all under the SIL Open Font License: neutral and engineered (Geist, Inter, IBM Plex Sans), technical (Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, Space Grotesk), geometric and round (Manrope, Sora, Outfit), wide and expressive (Unbounded, Syne), and serif (Fraunces, Instrument Serif, and Cinzel, which is cut from Roman inscriptions, the aqueduct's own era). Each is shown lowercase, in title case, and in spaced capitals.

The mark

F1

Three levels, thinning line

The direction you chose, as it stands: one arch, two, four, the line thinning as the arches get smaller.

F2

Two levels

One arch carrying two. Less detail, so it stays crisp at 16 px; the recursion is implied rather than shown.

F3

Monoline

Every arch drawn with the same line: more technical and more even, like a diagram.

F4

Solid

The arches as filled shapes: a half-disc, two holes, four half-discs again. Bold, works as a stamp and at 16 px.

F5

Water inside

No base line: the smallest arches are the water, as if what the structure carries runs through its finest parts.

F6

Four levels

One level deeper, for large use (a hero image, print, a T-shirt); paired with F1 or F2 for small sizes.
