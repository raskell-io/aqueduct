## How to sign

Comment on your pull request with exactly this sentence:

> I have read the Aqueduct CLA and I hereby sign it.

That comment is your electronic signature of this Agreement for the GitHub account that posts it. The
Project's CLA workflow records your GitHub login, account id, the time, this Agreement's version and
a link to the comment in the `cla-signatures` branch of the repository. You sign once; the Agreement
covers your present and future Contributions.

If you contribute on behalf of your employer, or your employer has rights to what you create, your
employer must sign the [Corporate CLA](CLA-CORPORATE.md) as well.
